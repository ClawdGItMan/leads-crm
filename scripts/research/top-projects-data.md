### Summary of Key Themes for Outreach

1. **NTT (Native Token Transfer) is the killer product.** Stablecoins and yield-bearing tokens (USDC, USDe, PYUSD, USDY, USD1, USDG, DAI) all need native multi-chain presence -- not wrapped tokens. NTT eliminates the "wrapped token" problem that causes fragmented liquidity.

2. **RWA tokenization is booming.** Ondo, Maker, Centrifuge, MANTRA, Plume, and Chainlink-connected projects all need cross-chain settlement for tokenized treasuries, gold, and credit products.

3. **L1/L2 bridging to Solana is the biggest opportunity.** Cardano just integrated LayerZero. Mantle is already on Solana. TON, Near, Cosmos, and Polkadot all have massive user bases that could bridge to SOL/SUI via Wormhole.

4. **DeFi protocols need cross-chain liquidity.** Aave, Uniswap, Morpho, Jupiter, Pendle, and Maple all operate across multiple chains but with fragmented liquidity. Wormhole messaging can unify this.

5. **Wormhole's existing partners (BlackRock BUIDL, VanEck, Apollo) create warm introductions** to projects those institutions are investing in -- especially Ondo, Morpho, and institutional DeFi protocols.

6. **The interoperability sector is small but strategic.** Axelar's dev team was acquired by Circle, LayerZero is integrating with Cardano, and Hyperlane is growing. Wormhole should position aggressively while competitors face transitions.
